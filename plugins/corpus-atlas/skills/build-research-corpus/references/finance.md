# Financial and company investigations

Prefer audited filings, regulator records, exchange notices, issuer disclosures, and original data.
Separate reporting period, filing date, announcement date, and market-observation time. Reconcile
GAAP or statutory figures with non-GAAP measures. Record currency, units, restatements, corporate
actions, and whether a figure is historical, guidance, consensus, or an analyst estimate. Treat price
movement as an observation, not proof of a causal explanation.
