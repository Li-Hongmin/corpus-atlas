# Public affairs, social issues, and news

Prefer laws, court records, government releases, public datasets, transcripts, direct statements,
and on-the-record field reporting. Separate what happened from what participants allege happened.
Preserve the event timeline and the publication/correction timeline. Seek independent corroboration,
identify circular reporting, and retain meaningful disagreement. Handle personal data minimally and
avoid amplifying unverified allegations about private individuals.
